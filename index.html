About

Aman Tripathi is a product leader working at the intersection of autonomous systems, robotics, and AI. Across eleven years in autonomy, mobility, and industrial technology, he has spent the last several years leading product for Wisk Aero's (a Boeing company) autonomous electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft program — setting the market requirements, business case, and go-to-market strategy behind a multi-year certification program. His work spans product strategy, systems requirements, and commercialization for safety-critical, regulated hardware, built on an early-career foundation in automotive and industrial manufacturing engineering.
